HOW TO PLAY

GOAL
Dive as deep as you can. Find the stairs on each floor. Every 5th floor a boss guards them: the Ember Lord, Bone King, Brood Mother, Void Eye and, deep in the Dimhearth, the Pale Flame, each tougher than the last. Bosses always drop 3 Rare-or-better items with a big Legendary chance. Gold-crowned elites drop better loot too, and beware chests that bite.

PHONE / TABLET
Drag anywhere on the left side to move. Hold ATK to attack the nearest foe. Tap your skill, POTION, BAG, or II to pause. The ↻ button turns the camera.

COMPUTER
WASD or arrows: move
Left click (hold) or Space/J: attack toward the mouse
Right click, K or E: class skill
Q: drink potion   I or Tab: bag   F: auto-attack
Z / C: turn the camera
Esc or P: pause, save & exit

LOOT
Grey < Green < Blue < Purple < Gold. Better gear rolls bonus perks. You can wear a weapon, helm, body armor, gloves, boots, an amulet and two rings. Everything you wear shows on your hero in its rarity color, and Epic and Legendary gear glows. Your class hits 25% harder with its favorite weapons.

THE DIMHEARTH
Below B20 the light runs thin. Gloom from ashen foes shrinks your sight and slows you. Walk into a hearthfire to light it and become Kindled: no Gloom, and ashen foes take more damage. Wickwraiths snuff fires, so light them again.

TRADERS
Look for the hooded trader marked with a gold $ (gold dot on the map). Walk into them to BUY gear, potions and bigger bags (up to 32 slots), or SELL loot for gold. Unwanted items can also be trashed from the bag. Enemies, chests and bosses drop gold.

TOWN
You start in Emberford. Walk up to townsfolk to talk: the Smith sells gear, the Healer heals you and your pet for free, the Sage identifies hidden items, the Enchanter adds powers to gear, and Elder Hobb gives quests. The gate at the top takes you down to your deepest depth. Press TOWN (or T) in the dungeon to open a portal home when no enemies are close.

YOUR PET
Cinder the fox fights beside you and carries 8 extra items when your bag is full. Open BAG to take items back or send Cinder to sell them. Feed it fish to change its form for 4 minutes.

FISHING
Find a pond, press FISH (or R), wait, and press again when it says REEL IN!

GEMS
Some gear has sockets (◇). Gems dropped by foes and chests go into them from the BAG.

SAVING
The game saves when you take the stairs, when you Save & Exit, and when you leave the page. Death is permanent: a fallen hero's slot is freed for a new run.
