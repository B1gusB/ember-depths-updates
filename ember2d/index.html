HOW TO PLAY

GOAL
Dive as deep as you can. Find the stairs on each floor. Every 5th floor a boss guards them: the Ember Lord, Bone King, Brood Mother, Void Eye and, deep in the Dimhearth, the Pale Flame, each tougher than the last. From depth 10 on, bosses grow with you: the stronger your hero, the more HP they have, so expect a real fight. Bosses always drop 3 Rare-or-better items with a big Legendary chance. Gold-crowned elites drop better loot too, and beware chests that bite.

PHONE / TABLET
Drag anywhere on the left side to move. Hold ATK to attack the nearest foe. Tap your skill, POTION, BAG, or II to pause.

COMPUTER
WASD or arrows: move
Left click (hold) or Space/J: attack toward the mouse
Right click, K or E: class skill
Q: drink potion   I or Tab: bag   F: auto-attack
Esc or P: pause, save & exit

LOOT
Grey < Green < Blue < Purple < Gold. Better gear rolls bonus perks. You can wear a weapon, helm, body armor, gloves, boots, an amulet and two rings. Your helm, gloves and boots show on your hero in their rarity color. Your class hits 25% harder with its favorite weapons.

THE DIMHEARTH
Below B20 the light runs thin. Gloom from ashen foes shrinks your sight and slows you. Walk into a hearthfire to light it and become Kindled: no Gloom, and ashen foes take more damage. Wickwraiths snuff fires, so light them again.

TRADERS
Look for the hooded trader marked with a gold $ (gold dot on the map). Walk into them to BUY gear, potions and bigger bags (up to 32 slots), or SELL loot for gold. Unwanted items can also be trashed from the bag. Enemies, chests and bosses drop gold.

SAVING
The game saves when you take the stairs, when you Save & Exit, and when you leave the page. Death is permanent: a fallen hero's slot is freed for a new run.
